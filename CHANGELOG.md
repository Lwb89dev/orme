# Changelog

All notable changes to Orme are documented here.

## [0.1.0] — 2026-10-16

### Added

- Complete English catalogue for the Wayfarer, Garden, Ways of Wisdom, Dialogue, journal, history, sources, and teachings.
- English app chrome with a language selector in Settings; English is the default for new installations.
- Editorial English translations preserving the solemn Catholic and Franciscan register of the Italian source material.
- Source badges, authenticity notices, editorial translation labels, and review warnings in English.
- Local journal export in English, including dates and headings.

### Integrity

- Original source texts remain preserved alongside their editorial translations.
- Editorial paraphrases remain explicitly labelled; no new quotations attributed to Francis were introduced.
- The English catalogue passes the shipped-content validator with no reference errors.

### Build

- Release APK signed with the Orme release keystore.
